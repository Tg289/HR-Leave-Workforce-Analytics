# Business Recommendations

These recommendations are based on the synthetic dataset and should be treated as portfolio-analysis examples rather than real HR policy.

1. **Monitor sustained attendance differences.** Departments with consistently lower attendance can be reviewed for recurring operational causes.

2. **Track leave concentration.** Employees or teams with unusually concentrated approved leave can be monitored alongside staffing requirements.

3. **Review leave-type mix.** Comparing paid and sick leave helps HR understand the composition of absence rather than looking only at total leave days.

4. **Centralize HR reporting.** A standardized data model and dashboard can reduce repeated manual consolidation.

5. **Use historical trends for planning.** Monthly leave patterns can inform staffing coverage and workload planning.

6. **Keep pending requests visible.** Pending leave should be separated from approved leave so workforce-availability calculations do not overstate confirmed absence.
