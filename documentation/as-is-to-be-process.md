# As-Is vs To-Be Process

## As-Is

1. Employees submit leave requests through manual channels.
2. HR records requests in separate files.
3. Attendance and leave information may be maintained separately.
4. HR manually consolidates data for reporting.
5. Management receives periodic reports with limited trend visibility.

## To-Be

1. Leave and employee records are standardized.
2. Attendance and leave data are centralized.
3. SQL performs repeatable data-quality checks and KPI calculations.
4. Power BI provides a management dashboard.
5. HR uses trends and department-level insights for workforce planning.

## Expected Improvements

- Less manual consolidation
- Consistent KPI definitions
- Faster reporting
- Better visibility into workforce availability
- Easier identification of recurring patterns
