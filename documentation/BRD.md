# Business Requirements Document

## 1. Project Overview

The HR Leave & Workforce Analytics project provides a centralized analytical view of employee leave and attendance information.

## 2. Business Problem

HR reporting can become time-consuming when leave and attendance information is maintained across manual records. Management needs a consistent way to monitor workforce availability and identify trends.

## 3. Objectives

- Reduce manual reporting effort
- Standardize HR KPIs
- Identify leave and attendance trends
- Support workforce planning with data

## 4. Stakeholders

- HR Manager
- HR Executive
- Department Managers
- Business/Operations Leadership

## 5. Scope

### In Scope
- Employee master data
- Leave records
- Attendance summary
- KPI reporting
- Department and monthly analysis
- Business recommendations

### Out of Scope
- Payroll processing
- Real-time biometric integration
- Employee performance appraisal
- Production HRIS integration

## 6. Success Measures

- Centralized HR reporting dataset
- Repeatable KPI calculations
- Faster monthly reporting
- Clear visibility into leave and attendance patterns
